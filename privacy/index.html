Privacy & Data Governance Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Emporium Capitals Institutional Holdings

1. Information Collection & Cryptographic Security

Emporium Capitals collects personal identifying information (full legal name, email address, phone number, and residential jurisdiction) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulatory compliance. All sensitive identity records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 protocols.

2. Blockchain & Transaction Telemetry

Public blockchain transactions (TX hashes, deposit addresses, and token amounts) are recorded in our immutable ledger. We do not link public wallet addresses with external marketing data or third-party tracking engines.

3. Non-Disclosure & Data Protection

We maintain strict non-disclosure obligations. Client investment records, portfolio yields, and capital balances are confidential and will never be monetized, sold, or shared with commercial entities.

4. Data Deletion & Account Closure

Investors may request the complete deletion of their account telemetry and personal identification documents upon full withdrawal of capital, subject to mandatory 5-year AML statutory retention requirements.
